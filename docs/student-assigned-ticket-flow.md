# Student assigned-ticket flow

Issue #15 consolidates student lab work into **My Tickets**. The former,
unreachable My Labs implementation and its electronic duplicate of the printed
Field Journal were removed.

Each assigned ticket now presents one visible lifecycle:

1. **Triage** — read the client request and record impact, urgency, role, station,
   assets, and the initial client exchange.
2. **Work** — perform the lab, keeping tool/action/observation detail in the
   printed Service Log and posting concise progress updates in Cinder.
3. **Communicate** — use Waiting or Escalated when blocked and record the reason.
4. **Verify** — complete the verification checklist and client-facing resolution,
   then submit the ticket for instructor verification.
5. **Signed off** — the instructor checks the printed entry and closes or returns
   the ticket with feedback.

The next-action card remains visible beside the ticket on school laptops and
moves into the normal reading flow on narrow screens. Every save area retains a
truthful saving, saved, or failed state.
